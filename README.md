# Salma ERP – ERPNext & Frappe Business Management System

## Overview

Salma ERP is a customized Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) solution built on the ERPNext platform using the Frappe Framework.

The system brings different business operations together within an integrated platform, helping manage business processes, maintain structured records, and improve operational efficiency through customized modules and workflows.

This project involves multiple business modules developed and customized collaboratively by the development team to address different business requirements.

This repository presents an overview of the Salma ERP project, its major functional areas, and my individual development contributions.

## Project Objectives

The project focuses on:

- Supporting different business operations through an integrated ERP system.
- Customizing ERPNext modules to meet specific business requirements.
- Streamlining business processes through workflows and automation.
- Maintaining organized business records and information.
- Improving operational visibility through reports and customized features.

## Project Scope – Business Modules

Salma ERP covers multiple business areas. The following are some of its functional modules:

- **HR Management:** Recruitment, employee information, and employee lifecycle management.
- **Payroll:** Payroll-related business operations.
- **Certificate Management:** Certificate-related functionality associated with the Stock module.
- **Dyeing Management:** Dyeing-related business operations.
- **Other Business Areas:** Additional modules and functionalities developed by the team.

*Note: This is a high-level overview of the project scope, not a complete list of all implemented features.*
